      FAQs

      What is the difference between the products?

      Multi Collagen Granules give you Type I, II and III collagen peptides in one serving. Bovine Collagen Granules are grass fed Type I and III collagen peptides. Multi Collagen Radiance is our most complete formula, with Type I, III, V and X peptides, Vitamin C, Zinc and a natural source of hyaluronic acid and elastin. Vegan Protein Powder is plant based and contains collagen forming amino acids.

      How much should I take?

      One serving a day. That is 12 g of Multi Collagen Granules or Bovine Collagen Granules, 10 g of Multi Collagen Radiance, or 25 g of Vegan Protein Powder. Always follow the directions on the pack.

      When is the best time to take it?

      Whenever suits your routine. Many people stir Multi Collagen Granules or Bovine Collagen Granules into their morning coffee. Multi Collagen Radiance is best in a cold drink or smoothie. Vegan Protein Powder works well in a smoothie or shake.
